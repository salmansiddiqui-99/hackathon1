# Workstation Specifications

This section details the recommended workstation specifications for developing humanoid robotics applications.

## Minimum Requirements

- CPU: Intel i7 or AMD Ryzen 7 (8+ cores)
- RAM: 32GB DDR4 or higher
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX 3080 or higher (for Isaac Sim)
- Storage: 1TB SSD

## Recommended Configuration

- CPU: Intel i9 or AMD Ryzen 9
- RAM: 64GB DDR4 or higher
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4090 (for intensive simulation)
- Storage: 2TB NVMe SSD
