# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This book bridges the gap between digital AI and physical embodiment, focusing on how to apply AI to control humanoid robots in simulations and real environments.

## About this Book

This interactive digital book covers essential topics in Physical AI, including:

- **ROS 2 Fundamentals**: Understanding the robotic nervous system
- **Simulation Tools**: Creating digital twins with Gazebo and Unity
- **NVIDIA Isaac**: The AI-robot brain for advanced robotics
- **Vision-Language-Action**: Integrating perception and action

## Learning Path

The book is structured in a progressive learning path from weeks 1-13, starting with foundational concepts and advancing to complex implementations. Each module builds upon the previous ones, providing a comprehensive understanding of humanoid robotics.

## Target Audience

This book is designed for AI researchers, robotics engineers, and developers interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence and physical robotics. Basic programming knowledge is assumed, with examples primarily in Python and C++.

## How to Use This Book

- Start with Module 1 and progress sequentially through the modules
- Complete the hands-on projects and assessments
- Refer to the hardware requirements section for setting up your development environment
- Work through the capstone project to integrate all concepts
